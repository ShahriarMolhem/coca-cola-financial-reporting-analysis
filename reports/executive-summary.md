# Executive Summary — The Coca-Cola Company (KO)

**Independent undergraduate accounting and financial reporting study | FY2021–FY2025**  
Shahriar Molhem · BBA Finance Student, University of Georgia

## The question
How much of reported profit converts into operating cash, and what unusual items and balance-sheet categories affect that comparison?

## Three findings
**1. Reported operating profitability improved:** FY2025 net operating revenues were $47.941bn (FY2024: $47.061bn), and operating income was $13.762bn (FY2024: $9.992bn). Other operating charges fell from $4.163bn to $1.261bn. The result is GAAP, not an adjusted normal-run-rate estimate.

**2. Earnings diverged from cash:** FY2025 *consolidated* net income was $13.137bn but net operating cash flow $7.408bn. The $5.729bn difference reflects noncash adjustments and substantial operating asset/liability cash movements. The cash flow statement shows a **−$7.208bn** change in operating assets and liabilities. Financial filing notes discuss a significant fairlife contingent consideration cash payment. The FY2024 IRS tax-litigation deposit also affects comparisons. Do not infer accounting fraud from the ratios alone.

**3. Liquidity and comprehensive income matter:** FY2025 current ratio was 1.46x compared with 1.03x in FY2024, but held-for-sale classifications complicate comparisons. Foreign-currency translation OCI moved from **−$2.893bn in FY2024** to **+$2.868bn in FY2025**.

## Portfolio skills
- Sourcing SEC 10-K statements and reconciling GAAP totals (Income Statement, CFO, Balance Sheet, OCI).
- Connecting accrual accounting and operating cash flow using the indirect method.
- Building an Excel model with formula-linked historical statements, liquidity and margin ratios.
- Writing an evidence-based summary and presenting accounting caveats in professional English.

**Limitations:** No nonpublic company data, management forecasting, professional audit or CPA assurance. Owner review of formulas and presentation is required before public publication.
