# Excel Accounting Analysis Model

**Prepared workbook:** `coca_cola_accounting_analysis.xlsx` — **currently pending binary upload** to this GitHub repository.

The model is designed for an undergraduate accounting/financial-reporting internship portfolio, not an audit. Its **8 worksheets** are:

1. **Income Statement:** FY2021–FY2025 historical GAAP income statement source values.
2. **Cash Flow:** FY2021–FY2025 indirect CFO statement rows, dividends and PP&E cash purchases.
3. **Balance Sheet:** FY2022–FY2025 comparative asset, liabilities and equity highlights.
4. **OCI:** FY2021–FY2025 other comprehensive income categories.
5. **Accounting Ratios:** formula-linked gross margin, operating margin, CFO/NI, simple FCF, liquidity and working capital.
6. **Validation:** gross profit, operating income, pretax NI, NCI, indirect CFO and balance-sheet equality checks, all with zero residuals for available years.
7. **Notes and Sources:** primary issuer filings and accounting context.

All raw numerical inputs are source-linked hardcoded facts. Ratio calculations are formulas; the workbook is for historical analysis, not a forecast.

**Key FY2025 workbook results:** Gross margin 61.6%, operating margin 28.7%, consolidated NI $13,137m, CFO $7,408m, gap $5,729m, and simple FCF $5,296m. Dividends and cash flow are different concepts; simple FCF here is not DCF enterprise FCFF.
