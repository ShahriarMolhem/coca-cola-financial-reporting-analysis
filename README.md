# Coca-Cola | Accounting & Financial Reporting Analysis (FY2021–FY2025)

**Independent undergraduate accounting portfolio case study**  
**Shahriar Molhem** · BBA Finance · University of Georgia, Terry College of Business

**Company analyzed: The Coca-Cola Company (NYSE: KO, SEC CIK 0000021344)**. This is **NOT Coca-Cola Consolidated, Inc. (NASDAQ: COKE, CIK 0000317540)**, a distinct bottling company.

> **Release status:** Research in progress. Repository stays **Private** until owner reviews the model and presentation and explicitly approves publication.

## Research question
How does accounting profitability compare with actual cash conversion, and what do balance-sheet changes, operating charges, and other comprehensive income disclose about financial reporting over FY2021–FY2025?

## Planned workflow
1. PR #1 — Research design, primary SEC 10-K sources, reconciliation standards and project boundaries.
2. PR #2 — Five years of original reported GAAP income and operating cash flow, 2022–2025 comparative balance sheets, and validation checks.
3. PR #3 — Accrual-to-cash reconciliation, margins, working capital / liquidity, OCI, an Excel accounting analysis workbook and quality review.
4. PR #4 — Executive summary, 10-slide PowerPoint and first-person presentation script; release checklist and owner review.
