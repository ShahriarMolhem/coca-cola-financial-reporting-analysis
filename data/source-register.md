## Recording conventions
- Use **USD millions** except earnings per share and explicit percentage calculations.
- Income Statement **Consolidated Net Income** includes noncontrolling interests; **Net Income Attributable to Shareowners** excludes noncontrolling interests. Clearly label which numerator is used.
- CFO is **Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities**. Simple FCF is CFO **less purchases of property, plant and equipment**, *not* an unlevered FCFF DCF measure.
- Current ratio uses **reported total current assets divided by total current liabilities** and includes assets and liabilities held for sale. Interpret carefully.
- The FY2025 change in operating assets and liabilities includes significant **fairlife contingent consideration cash payments** (Note 21). FY2024 includes a significant **IRS tax litigation deposit**. Comparisons must disclose these.
- A cash-flow-to-net-income ratio is *descriptive* and should not on its own be presented as proof of accounting manipulation or a fraud risk.
- Keep other operating charges as their **actual GAAP classification**; do not silently delete or classify the whole line as nonrecurring.
- Values in the spreadsheet must link to relevant source URLs and preserve historical GAAP as reported.

## Verification baseline
The main income statement identity is Revenue – COGS = Gross Profit; Gross Profit – SG&A – Other Operating Charges = Operating Income; Pretax Income – Income Taxes = Consolidated Net Income. The main balance-sheet identity is Assets = Liabilities + Equity. The cash-flow reconciliation starts from **consolidated**, not attributable, net income.
